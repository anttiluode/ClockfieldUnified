# Morgue

### "Slow clocks automatically attract signals"

No. A local time scale by itself need not change branch probabilities. In the wave experiment, clock mass matters because it changes resonance and impedance together with the spatial coupling network.

### "This is gravitational attention"

No. The mass matrix is oscillator inertia. No Einstein equation, stress-energy tensor, gravitational field equation, or equivalence-principle claim appears here.

### "Physics routing is free on a GPU"

No. The implementation explicitly solves complex linear systems. A physical analog substrate might obtain propagation from its material dynamics; this NumPy version pays computation to simulate it.

### "SPACE_CLOCK beats a digital router"

Not established and not expected on this toy. A tiny explicit rule that knows frequency can route perfectly. The gate asks whether one fixed body can perform content-dependent routing without executing an input-dependent branch selector.

### "Clock mass alone is enough"

Not on Gate 0. CLOCK_ONLY reaches 75% held-out accuracy and has negative mean worst-frequency margin. The joint body reaches 93.75% and positive +3.30 dB mean worst-frequency margin.

### "Spatial coupling alone is enough"

Sometimes a seed finds a perfect routing solution, but across equal-budget scalar training SPACE_ONLY averages 81.25% and negative mean worst-frequency margin. Joint space+clock is substantially more reliable.

### "The body learned from local biological plasticity"

No. Gate 0 uses external SPSA driven by a scalar task score. A future gate must derive local updates to g and m from traffic plus global consequence.


### "Gate 0 only memorized four pure frequencies"

Too strong. Gate 1 freezes the Gate-0 bodies and evaluates 512 unseen mixtures of finite Gaussian-windowed packets with randomized phase, amplitude, width, onset, and +/-0.025 frequency jitter.

SPACE_CLOCK averages 94.24% correct routed components and +1.61 dB mean 5th-percentile margin. No Gate-1 retraining occurs.

### "Finite-packet success means nonlinear source separation"

No. The body is linear. Superposition plus frequency-selective transfer explains the result. The components are deliberately separated by carrier frequency. This is passive spectral routing, not blind source separation.

### "The joint body is robust on every trained seed"

No. Mean performance is strong, but some joint seeds still collapse to about 74.9% component accuracy and negative worst-case margin. Gate 1 establishes an across-seed advantage, not universal convergence.
